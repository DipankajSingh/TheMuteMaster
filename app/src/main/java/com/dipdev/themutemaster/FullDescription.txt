FAQ

Does it restore my volume when I leave a zone?
Yes — automatically restored the moment your device exits the geofence radius.

Will it drain my battery?
No. It uses Android's own optimized sensor-fusion geofencing — not active GPS polling.

Can I mute only media without silencing calls?
Yes. Each zone gives you independent control over ringer, notification, and media channels.

How many zones can I create?
Unlimited. Work, campus, gym, cinema, home, client offices — add as many locations as you need.

Is my location data shared with anyone?
Never. Location processing is 100% local. No servers or location tracking. We only optionally collect anonymized app usage analytics, which you can disable in settings at any time.
